## **People Also Ask**

### **1) Which brand is best for a home EV charger in Telangana?**

In general the best answer for home charging is a simple AC charger with good service support. Currently, the most prominent names in the source set for that use case are Exicom and SpiderEV. Exicom’s official EV charging page lists home solutions such as a remote management app, while SpiderEV’s current product stack includes 3.3 kW and 7.4 kW AC chargers and a home program built around standard home connections. The right question on a house or flat is not “who has the biggest charger?” It is “who can install it and support it and maintain it cleanly?” That's why home charging is a services game first, and a hardware game second.

### **2) Which brand is best if I want a fast DC charger for a commercial site?**

If your project needs commercial DC, the biggest names to consider are Delta, Servotech, Tata Power and SpiderEV. Delta’s official portfolio covers DC 25-200 kW and AC 7-22 kW, which is good for mixed commercial deployments. Prices on Servotech’s DC fast-charger page start from ₹3.5 lakh and go up to ₹25 lakh. Recent coverage says its 60kW and 120kW DC fast chargers got BEE 5-star ratings. SpiderEV has DC chargers from 30 kW to 240 kW. Tata Power claims its network has DC fast charging, with the capability of charging a vehicle in as little as 30 minutes. If you’re buying for a site, the right choice will depend on load, footfall and the type of EVs you expect.

### **3) What are the best fast DC charging options in Hyderabad right now?**

The current source set's best city options are GLIDA Hyderabad Airport Hub 120 kW, Sandhya Techno-1 2 × 120 kW, Silver Star – Sanath Nagar 120 kW, Lakshmi Hyundai 60 kW, Basheer Bagh EV Charger 60 kW, Tata Power- Kun Hyundai (60 kW). Tata Power is one of the safest franchise names, given the network scale, support and a clear partner model.
