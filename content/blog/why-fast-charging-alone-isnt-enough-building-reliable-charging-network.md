# **Why Fast Charging Alone Isn't Enough: Building a Reliable Charging Network**

A 120kW cabinet on a quiet patch looks like infrastructure. For drivers who arrive to a dark screen, a missing gun or a payment app that never begins the session, the opposite is true. That’s why networks fail in public more often than they fail because they lack peak power.

“how to build EV charging network” this search term’s work starts with that distinction. One of the tools is fast charging.” The product is reliable.

### **Quick Answer Box**

Fast DC chargers don’t form a network by themselves. A usable network mixes destination AC and corridor DC, matches authorised load to actual demand, runs OCPP monitoring, stocks wear parts and measures session success. Public charging is de-licensed in India but operators need land, a DISCOM EV connection and, in Telangana, TGREDCO single-window clearance and TGSPDCL or TGNPDCL load sanction. The indicative set-up cost varies from a few lakhs for small AC sites to ₹15-25 lakh and above for DC hubs. Capital earns depending on utilisation and uptime.

## **What “Network” Means in Practice**

A network is a set of locations a driver can trust on a trip or a workday. Trust requires:

- [ ] The bay is findable in an app or on the site.
- [ ] The connector matches the vehicle.
- [ ] Power actually flows.
- [ ] Payment or access completes.
- [ ] A second gun or a nearby site exists if the first fails.

Nameplate kilowatts help only after those conditions are true. A 60 kW unit that completes sessions beats a 240 kW unit that is offline three days a week.

## **Why Fast Charging Is Necessary but Incomplete**

DC fast charging is the right answer for highway legs, taxis between trips and fleets that can’t wait four hours. It is the wrong answer as the only asset class because:

Most of the passenger energy goes to AC due to long stays at home, offices and apartments. 7.4-22 kW destination sites have cheaper sessions and less grid shock. Public DC utilisation has been at or below commonly cited operating break-even in many Indian samples. Demand charges penalise idle high-power connections. Cables, connectors and communications fail more often than marketing slides would have us believe .

Operators that buy fast chargers only focus capex where utilisation is hardest to fill and ignore the sites where vehicles already park for hours.

## **The Layers of a Reliable Network**

**1. Site logic, not charger logic**

Start with who is parking, for how long and how they find the bay. Highway fuel retail, mall parking, IT-park decks and fleet depots are different products. Networks copy-paste DC into all four to collect dead assets.

**2. Hardware mix**

AC for dwell. DC for turnover. Dual guns where possible so one fault does not kill the site. CCS2 as the four-wheeler public default. Separate two- and three-wheeler points where that demand is real.

**3. Grid before glory**

The cabinet’s ability to deliver its rating depends on the sanctioned load, transformer headroom, earthing and surge protection. In Telangana, loads above modest commercial thresholds draw dedicated transformers and clearances from the electrical inspector. You pay for a 150 kW charger on a weak feeder, you get derating and trips, not speed.

**4. Software as the control room**

OCPP-compliant chargers report to a charge point management system with remote reboot, firmware, pricing, occupancy and fault codes. When the unit is online, a large percentage of DC faults can be cleared without a truck roll. A dumb heater with a brand panel, is a fast charger that can't talk.

**5. Operations**

Weekly cable checks, monthly connector care, monsoon earthing tests, spare guns on the shelf and a named person who owns uptime. Networks without this layer become the offline pins drivers screenshot .

**6. Discovery and access**

Listing on major apps, clear signage, working payment and a simple start flow. Reliability includes the minute before the session, not only the kilowatts during it.

## **How to Build an EV Charging Network in India**

Public charging is a de-licensed activity in terms of the[ MoP guidelines](https://powermin.gov.in/). This requires a licence barrier. It doesn’t take away land power and operations.

Sequence that stands up in the field:

- Site lock control (own, lease, host agreement or franchise site) .
- Measure the electrical distance to the network and estimate the allowable load.
- The AC/DC mix should come from dwell time not from a catalogue.
- Apply for DISCOM connection under EV charging tariff category.
- Complete electrical & electrical inspector safety clearance where required.
- OCPP hooked up to a live backend for commission hardware.
- Publish the site, train host staff and get the maintenance calendar rolling from day one.

Central schemes like PM E-DRIVE can support upstream [infrastructure for eligible public sites ](https://www.niti.gov.in/)through state nodal agency. The windows change. Design the site to be able to work without subsidy, then if open layer in incentive.

## **EV Charging Station Setup Cost in India**

Price will depend on the site. Indicative planning bands for 2026:

- Small AC workplace or destination cluster: typically ₹2–12 lakh, depending on count and electrical work.
- Mid commercial mix (AC + 1 DC + civil / electrical): common ₹15-25 lakh
- High power highway or multi-gun DC hub: from ₹25 lakh to well over ₹1 crore, when transformers and long cable runs are involved.

The hardware is only part of the stack. The cabinet sticker does not change the total. Transformer and service upgradation, civil pad, earthing, software, security deposit with the DISCOM and first year maintenance.

## **How to Apply for an EV Charging Station in Telangana**

Public charging does not require a separate electricity licence at a national level.

The practical way in Telangana is;

- Use the single-window process of TGREDCO for clearance of EV charging station (the state nodal route)
- Apply TGSPDCL or TGNPDCL for load under EV charging tariff category with site details and for larger loads transformer and inspector requirements.
- Keep Land Documents, Site Photos, Charger Test or Certification Papers, and Identity or Company Papers as the portal demands.
- Protect, commission and complete earthing before making the site public.

With a large volume of single-window approvals under its belt, TGREDCO remains the first port of call for operators who want a documented state path. TGSPDCL has also conducted facilitation drives for public charging connections for EVs. Check current fees, list of documents and EoIs for subsidy on the live portals before filing. Policy numbers change a lot more often than blog posts.

A lot of the state’s operational public charging is already in Hyderabad. Feeder strength as much as charger kW still dictates corridor and industrial sites outside the core.

Charging is de-licensed is the national rule as per Ministry of Power guidelines. NITI Aayog’s mobility planning views public, workplace and home charging as an integrated system. Central Electricity Authority –[ Electrical safety expectations](https://cea.nic.in/). The Telangana Electricity Regulatory Commission determines the category of tariff that EV operators actually pay.

| Success metric | Installed kW | Session success and uptime |

Operators who publish a map of offline pins teach drivers to avoid the whole brand. Operators only add capacity where there are existing sessions and keep fewer working sites build the asset drivers return to.

Reliability is part of redundancy. One trophy cabinet beats two moderate guns. When the highway DC site is full, overflow is sent to a destination AC cluster five minutes off the corridor. The charging should be treated as electrical infrastructure, both within the framework of the India Energy Storage Alliance C&I energy and the Bureau of Indian [Standards certification](https://www.bis.gov.in/). The same reliability argument holds true globally for EA’s public charging work. Optional on-site storage can reduce demand charges at DC hubs, but it’s no substitute for uptime discipline.

## **People Also Ask**

### **How do you build an EV charging network in India?**

Manage the site, size the electrical connection, select AC/DC mix from dwell time, get a DISCOM [EV-tariff connection,](https://www.tgerc.telangana.gov.in/) commission OCPP-connected hardware and run a maintenance and discovery process from day one.” Charging publicly is unlicensed. The land, the load, the uptime are still hard work.

### **What does it cost to set up an EV charging station in India?**

Indicative bands: Small AC clusters often ₹2-12 lakh; mid AC-plus-DC commercial sites commonly ₹15-25 lakh; high-power DC hubs ₹25 lakh to over ₹1 crore when transformers and long cable runs are required. Always include deposits and the first year operations in the software. On site electrical work puts it over the charger brochure.

### **How to apply for an EV charging station in Telangana?**

Apply for load to TGSPDCL or TGNPDCL on EV charging category. File through [TGREDCO’s single-window clearance for EV charging stations.](http://tsev.telangana.gov.in/ev_charging_station_load_en.php) Prepare land papers, site photographs and charger certification as stated in portal. For larger loads, you need transformer work and electrical-inspector clearance. Check the live document and fee lists before submission.

### **Why is fast charging not enough on its own?**

Most of the daily energy is delivered during the long dwell on AC. Typically, there is little public DC usage. High power connections are charged a demand charge when not used. Communication and connectors fail. A network that sells only speed without uptime, discovery and a second available gun, loses drivers.

### **What utilisation do public DC sites need?**

Break-even depends on tariff, demand charges and capex, but many analyses treat mid-teens utilisation as the zone where operating costs start to be covered. Top urban sites run higher. Weak corridor sites run lower. Underwrite the specific plot, not the national average.

### **What software does a network need?**

An OCPP-compliant charge-point management system for status, remote reboot, pricing, user access and fault logs. Without it, every fault is a site visit and every price change is a truck roll.

### **Should every site be DC fast charging?**

No. Offices, apartments, hotels and malls with multi-hour parking should lead with AC. DC belongs on highways, fuel retail and high-turnover fleet or taxi locations. Mixed sites often outperform single-speed sites.
