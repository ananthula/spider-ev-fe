# **How EV Charging Can Increase Footfall for Shopping Centres**

For a family in an electric SUV from Gachibowli, the weekend doesn't start with a query about which mall has the latest store. They want to know what destination can they park, plug in, eat and shop at without a[ second stop at a highway charger](https://spiderenergy.in/blog/highway-ev-charging-investment-india). If one Hyderabad center has live charger availability and the other does not, the first center wins the trip before a single retail banner is seen. Here’s how ev charging can boost footfall for shopping centers: it steals a planning decision that used to belong only to cinema listings and food-court reputation.

Time is already sold in shopping centers. Parking, air conditioning, entertainment and dining are there to keep people on the site long enough to spend. EV charging is the same concept with a battery attached. The driver has a good reason to stay. They have a real reason to have them at the mall.

**Quick Answer Box**

Visible, working EV charging tailored to visit length increases footfall. AC destination chargers suit the average mall dweller. Drivers needing a rapid top-up will appreciate DC fast chargers. Most mall chargers in India are paid through operator apps. Some sites still offer a promotional offer of free or bill-validated sessions. The commercial prize is not just the electricity margin. It’s incremental time and spend.

## **Why Charging Changes the Trip Decision**

Footfall is a competition between destinations. EV owners introduce yet another filter: can I add range, all the while spending two hours inside?

Once you have that filter, a mall with no chargers isn’t neutral. It is not over. Drivers living in apartments without good home charging, or mid-trip across the Outer Ring Road, will prefer the center that solves parking and energy together.

This is called destination charging. The visit is not “wait for a charge. It is shopping, eating out, watching a film or going to an event with energy being added to the background. People usually spend one to three hours at Indian malls. In that window, a 7 kW to 11 kW AC session can replace a standard day of driving around the city. DC units of 50 kW to 180 kW serve shoppers who will linger [20 to 45 minutes or show up with a low battery](https://www.zevpoint.com/blogs/guides/ev-charging-shopping-malls-retail-india).

International retail-charging studies report the highest [utilisation rates at grocery and mall sites](https://theevreport.com/retail-ev-charging-grocery-utilization), well above the network median. Malls are high use and relatively under-built as a share of all stations, an opening for owners who move first in a trade area.

## **How EV Charging Can Increase Traffic for Shopping Centers Near Hyderabad, Telangana**

Hyderabad is an abnormally good market for this amenity.

The city has dense IT corridors, increasing four-wheeler EV ownership, flat living with patchy home charging and weekend destination retail in HITEC City, Gachibowli, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Kukatpally, and the growing west and south catchments. Public charging has already appeared in those corridors, shopping malls and commercial parking. Networks mapped for the city include Tata Power EZ Charge, ChargeZone, Statiq, Zeon and others[ with a mix of AC and DC points.](https://www.electricpe.com/ev-charging-stations-hyderabad)

And local proof points keep coming. Tesla launched destination AC charging inside a Delhi mall parking level as a “plug in, charge, and go” shopping companion. In Hyderabad, operators have installed DC fast chargers in mall environments such as MPM Times Square, combining 120 kW and 180 kW units with café, restaurant, cinema and shopping amenities. Tata Power and Tata Passenger Electric Mobility have also opened the first high-speed mega hub for Telangana in Hitec City and highway points have been added on routes feeding the Hyderabad weekend catchment. Drivers already see charging as part of a stop for food and retail. A mall that offers the same bundle keeps that [spend within its four walls.](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/auto/tesla-launches-north-india-s-first-in-mall-destination-charging-station-in-delhi/story)

The [Telangana policy support is critical for owners and drivers](https://bwautoworld.com/article/centre-issues-guidelines-for-72-300-ev-charging-stations-under-pm-e-drive-573477-574497) as well. The state has also used demand-side EV incentives, such as extended road-tax and registration relief. At the national level, the guidelines for the operational support of PM E-Drive consider shopping malls and market complexes as eligible sites for the upstream infrastructure support, which can reduce the capital barrier to providing adequate electrical capacity to the parking floor.

The traffic thesis for a Hyderabad owner is specific: intercept EV households in Financial District, Madhapur, Kondapur and Jubilee Hills who already drive to lifestyle centers, and intercept ORR and highway users who will prefer a mall to a lonely charger if they can make the wait a lunch moment.

## **Are EV Charging Stations in Malls Free?**

Short answer: not most of the time. Some are free for a limited time, or with purchase. Most of the public mall chargers in India are paid.

Drivers will start a session within an operator app such as Tata Power EZ Charge, ChargeZone, Statiq or Zeon. Hyderabad indicative public tariffs in 2026 are in the range of ₹8-14/kWh for AC and ₹15-22/kWh for DC, excluding any parking fee that the mall itself may charge. The [rate depends on the operator, ](https://www.carbikegpt.com/electric-charging-station/hyderabad)location and time of day.

Complimentary models still exist as marketing, not as the national default:

- [ ] Time-capped free AC sessions to attract weekday traffic
- [ ] Validation: free or discounted units after a minimum bill from the food court or an anchor store
- [ ] Tenant or staff tariffs that differ from public pay-as-you-go
- [ ] Occasional dealership or hotel comps that should not be confused with mall public policy

Some of the older city round-ups have talked about purchase-linked complimentary charging at select Hyderabad malls. These offers may change without notice. “The owners have to have the current rule published on wayfinding and in the operator app, not just take word of mouth,” he said.

Free charging does not automatically mean more footfall. Uncapped free DC sparks bay blocking, queues, and angry combustion car parkers. More than that, the mall benefit: validated discount, loyalty points or first 30 mins free on AC if the driver spends in-centre, is paid energy

The language around government subsidies can be confusing in this respect. As part of the PM E-Drive, chosen government-owned sites could be asked to provide publicly accessible chargers, and in certain cases, free to use chargers, so as to get the full equipment subsidy. Private shopping malls[ are considered high-traffic commercial sites](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/govt-issues-guidelines-to-set-up-ev-charging-stations-under-pm-e-drive-125092800700_1.html) that are entitled to upstream support. That's not the same thing as a mandate that mall owners give away electricity. Do not commit to free public charging as a policy without checking the latest conditions of the scheme with the implementing agency.

## **The Commercial Mechanics: Dwell Time, Not Just Kilowatt Hours**

On a well-run mall on a P&L basis, the electricity sale is the smallest line item.

A typical three-hour 7 kW AC session might use about 20 kWh. Driver pays a few hundred rupees at the standard Indian public AC tariffs. The operator margin per session is small after the cost of power. A busy bay running multiple sessions a day can still generate useful cash, and a revenue share with a charge-point operator (often in the 15-30 percent band for the property, depending on the deal) can fund the amenity with limited capex.

Dwell is the larger number. Destination charging gives the visitor a reason to complete a food-court meal, browse a second floor or add a cinema. Retail studies of EV chargers at stores and malls report longer stays and incremental spend when the charger is a short walk from shopping and dining. Exact lifts depend on format and implementation. Treat vendor claims of “50 extra minutes” as directional and measure your own baseline: arrival time, first dwell, categories visited, and ticket size for charger users versus general parkers.

Adding experiences already increases dwell by 25-35 percent, a mall entertainment strategy. Charging is a more muted cousin to that strategy. This is not a theme park. It is the [infrastructure that makes the current experience easier ](https://spiderenergy.in/blog/charging-infrastructure-trends-2026-india)to choose.

## **How Mall Owners Should Design the Amenity**

Placement is better than poster campaigns. Place bays on the parking level closest to F&B and main mall doors, with painted wayfinding from the entry ramps. Low utilisation and high vandalism from hidden basements.

Speeds vary. A Hyderabad weekend center could begin with a handful of 7–11 kW AC destination points for the long shoppers and at least one dual-gun DC unit for the drivers who will not be staying two hours. Bays keep turning with idle fees or app alerts after session complete.

Up-time is a reputation. A dead charger with a “available” sign teaches drivers to trust the rival mall. Service level agreements with the operator should include remote monitoring, spare parts response and public uptime target.

Loyalty, too. A kWh discount on a food-court bill is valid, or bonus points on the mall app, linking energy to spend, not just giving energy away blindly.

Plan for power early. Transformers and fire-and-parking approvals take longer than the pedestal mounting for upstream capacity. Here is where scheme support and DISCOM coordination matter more than brochure charger numbers.

Decide the operating model before procurement:

- [ ] Mall-owned hardware and tariff (control and capex)
- [ ] Operator-funded hardware with revenue share (speed and brand discovery)
- [ ] Hybrid: mall funds civil and power, operator funds chargers and app

Tesla-style destination charging and CPO mall installs in India show both paths can work if the driver experience is simple: find bay, start session, walk inside.

## **Risk Analysis for Retail Owners**

Risks include bay hogging, combustion vehicles parked in EV stalls, low uptime, tariff confusion across apps and electrical upgrades that stall the project. Another risk is treating charging as CSR signs. Drivers are faster to punish an unreliable site than they are to reward a green plaque.

Operational mitigation: bays painted and enforced, idle fees, 24/7 monitoring, single recommended app plus QR start, quarterly review of sessions, dwell and tenant sales near the charging cluster.

Don’t build too much on day one. Two good dual standards are better than 8 dead pedestals.

Generic advice buys hardware. Strategic thinking buys trips.

## **People Also Ask**

### **Q: How can EV charging increase footfall for shopping centres?**

It makes the mall a planned stop for EV owners who need parking and range. Visible, working chargers pull trips that would otherwise go to another centre, an office park, or a highway plaza, then convert charging time into shopping and dining.

### **Q: How can EV charging increase traffic for shopping centers near Hyderabad, Telangana?**

EV drivers in Hyderabad already charge at malls, IT parks and new high-speed hubs. A center listing live bays, close to F&B and working on weekends can intercept Financial District and west Hyderabad households plus ORR travellers.

### **Q: Are EV charging stations in malls free?**

Most public mall chargers in India are paid through operator apps. A minority of sites offer time-limited free AC charging or discounts with a purchase receipt. Always check the live tariff in the app.

### **Q: What does charging usually cost at a Hyderabad mall?**

Public sessions commonly fall around ₹8–14 per kWh on AC and ₹15–22 per kWh on DC, [depending on the network and site](https://spiderenergy.in/blog/future-ev-charging-networks-india-what-matters). Parking charges may apply separately.

### **Q: Should a mall install AC or DC chargers?**

Install both if budget allows. AC destination charging fits long shopping trips. DC fast charging raises map visibility and serves short-stay drivers. Start with a small reliable mix rather than a single extreme.

### **Q: How do malls make money from EV charging?**

Through energy margin or operator revenue share, and more importantly through longer dwell, F&B tickets, repeat visits, and a premium brand position. Direct kWh profit is usually secondary.

### **Q: Are there subsidies for mall charging in India?**

PM E-Drive guidelines include shopping malls and market complexes among sites that can receive support on upstream infrastructure. Terms, percentages, and conditions should be verified with the implementing agency at the time of application.
